# Carbonly User Guide & Platform Handbook

## 1. Introduction
Carbonly makes tracking and reducing carbon footprints simple, transparent, and actionable—without requiring a PhD in government climate regulations.

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard
The Carbonly Dashboard is divided into 4 subpage panels:

### Panel 1: Overview & Historical Progress
- **Total Footprint KPI**: Displays your current operational carbon emissions in kg CO2e and metric tons ($tCO_2e$).
- **Human Scope Categories**:
  - *Direct Driving & Fuel*: Vehicle transport mileage and fuel combustion.
  - *Home & Office Power*: Regional electricity grid draw.
  - *Travel, Water & Digital*: Flight travel, water usage, and internet data transfer.
- **Historical Progress Chart**: Displays your month-over-month emissions trend so you can track whether your footprint is improving over time.
- **12-Month Time-Series Forecast**: Shows predicted future emissions under business-as-usual vs. Net Zero targets.

### Panel 2: Activity Calculator & Sandbox
- Enter your transport distance, electricity kWh, flight counts, and water consumption.
- Click **"Load Pre-Loaded Sample Profiles"** to instantly test datasets for Tech Offices, Logistics Fleets, or Global Consulting Firms.

### Panel 3: What-If Scenario Simulator
- Adjust scenario sliders (Vehicle Reduction, Solar PPA, Flight Consolidation) to model potential carbon and cost savings.
- Use the **"Auto-Optimize for My Budget"** solver to find the optimal combination of actions for your target annual budget.

### Panel 4: AI Strategy Hub & Copilot
- Review prioritized action cards sorted by carbon ROI.
- Ask custom questions to the Groq AI Copilot Assistant regarding energy efficiency, EV transitions, or grid purchasing.
