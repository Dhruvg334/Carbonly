Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Carbonly prevent AI calculation hallucinations? ▼
Carbonly strictly separates deterministic math from LLM language generation. All emission factors ($kg CO_2e$) are calculated via validated JavaScript math routines using UK DEFRA and US EPA tables. The Groq LLM is used solely for narrative summaries and Q&A assistance.
What is the difference between Direct Driving, Power Grid, and Travel? ▼
Direct Driving represents Scope 1 direct fuel emissions from vehicles. Home & Office Power represents Scope 2 indirect electricity emissions. Travel, Water & Digital represents Scope 3 value-chain activities.
Can I export formal reports for ESG compliance? ▼
Yes! Logged-in users can click "Export ESG Report" on the Overview panel of the Dashboard to immediately download a certified Markdown/HTML ESG Audit Report detailing category footprints, EcoScore benchmarks, and methodology references.
