Why Track Carbon Footprint?

Tracking our carbon footprint is essential in understanding the environmental impact of our daily actions. Every choice we make — from transportation to energy consumption — contributes to global emissions.

By individually monitoring and reducing our footprint, we create collective impact. Small consistent actions multiply into meaningful environmental change.

This platform empowers individuals to become climate-conscious decision makers and actively participate in building a sustainable future.

About Carbonly

Carbonly is a web-based platform designed to help individuals monitor and reduce their carbon footprint by tracking their weekly lifestyle activities.

Users enter information such as transportation distance, electricity usage, water consumption, and air travel. The system processes this data and estimates carbon emissions using a machine learning model.

The platform then visualizes carbon trends and provides AI-based recommendations to help users adopt more sustainable habits.

This project integrates modern web technologies, machine learning, and data analytics to promote environmental awareness and responsible living.
