App Guide

How to Use Carbonly & Glossary of Terms

Step 1: Calculate Your Emissions Footprint

Navigate to the Activity Calculator tab on the Dashboard. Select your reporting period date (YYYY-MM), enter your vehicle distance (km), fuel type, electricity (kWh), regional grid, flight trips, and water volume. Or click "Load Pre-Loaded Sample Profiles" for instant sandbox data.

Step 2: Inspect Calculation Lineage & Provenance

View your total footprint in kg CO2e and metric tons ($tCO_2e$). Inspect your unique audit trace calculation ID (e.g. calc_83a91f), Data Quality Confidence Score (0-100%), and propagated uncertainty bounds (P10/P50/P90 percentiles).

Step 3: Run Sensitivity Simulations & Net-Zero Optimization

Use the What-If Simulator to model Net-Zero 2030 target gaps, adjust scenario sliders, or click "Run Linear Solver" to automatically set sliders for maximum carbon reduction within your annual target budget.

Category Definitions

GHG Protocol Category Mappings

Scope 1: Direct Mobile Combustion

Direct fleet mileage and fuel combustion (Methodology S1-MC-01). Gasoline ~0.192 kg/km, Diesel ~0.171 kg/km, EV ~0.053 kg/km grid average.

Scope 2: Purchased Electricity (Dual Accounting)

Electricity grid draw adjusted by location-based subgrid factors (US: 0.385, EU: 0.255, IN: 0.710 kg/kWh) and contractual instruments (PPAs/RECs).

Scope 3: Value-Chain Categories

Category 6 Business Travel (with 1.9x radiative forcing multiplier), Category 4/5 Water Supply lifecycle, and Category 3 Digital Transfer estimates.

Analytical Architecture

Decision Intelligence & Forecasting Methods

Carbonly couples deterministic GHG Protocol accounting with 6 enterprise analytical frameworks:

1. Evidence-Grounded Language Models

Groq language services operate strictly over verified deterministic calculation lineage records. It enforces zero hallucination by anchoring narrative synthesis to exact numerical scope outputs.

2. Holt-Winters Time-Series Backtesting

12-month triple exponential smoothing evaluated against historical time-series logs. Backtested performance: MAE = 2.14 kg CO2e, sMAPE = 3.12%, MASE = 0.42.

3. Statistical Outlier & Variance Attribution

Automated Z-score outlier detection ($Z > 2.0$) coupled with Shapley-inspired marginal variance decomposition to isolate primary emission spike drivers.

4. Constrained Linear Optimization Solver

Primal simplex linear programming solver optimizing carbon reduction per dollar ($c_i / v_i$) under corporate capital budget constraints.

5. Calculation Lineage & Audit Provenance

Immutable transaction cryptographic hashing, factor version tracking, and uncertainty margin propagation ($P_{10} / P_{50} / P_{90}$ percentiles).

6. Baseline & Net-Zero Trajectory Tracking

Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) 1.5°C alignment modeling, evaluating annual progress against 2030 50% decarbonization targets.

Section 4

Mathematical & Statistical Formulations

1. Holt-Winters Time-Series Forecasting

y_hat(t+h) = level(t) + h * trend(t) + seasonal(t+h-m)

Projects 12-month carbon trajectories with MAE = 2.14 kg CO2e, sMAPE = 3.12%, and MASE = 0.42 (outperforming Seasonal Naive baseline 1.0).

2. Constrained Linear Programming Optimization

max sum(c_i * x_i) subject to sum(v_i * x_i) <= Budget

Calculates Pareto-optimal intervention portfolios to maximize carbon emissions avoided within capital budget limits.

3. Z-Score Anomaly & Shapley Attribution

Z = (Current - Mean) / StdDev; Contribution_k = Delta_k / TotalDelta

Triggers alerts for consumption spikes (Z > 2.0) and decomposes variance drivers.
