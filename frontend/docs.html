App Guide

How to Use Carbonly & Glossary of Terms

Step 1: Calculate Your Emissions Footprint

Navigate to the Activity Calculator tab on the Dashboard. Enter your vehicle distance, fuel type, electricity kWh, regional grid, flight count, and water usage. Or click "Load Pre-Loaded Sample Profiles" for instant sandbox data.

Step 2: Inspect EcoScore & Historical Trends

View your total footprint in kg CO2e and metric tons ($tCO_2e$). Inspect your relative EcoScore (0-1000 pts) and 5-Star rating. Check your Historical Progress Line Chart to see if your footprint is improving over time!

Step 3: Run Sensitivity Simulations & Optimization

Use the What-If Simulator to adjust scenario sliders, or click "Run Linear Solver" to automatically set the sliders for maximum carbon reduction within your annual target budget.

Category Definitions

Human Category Mappings

Direct Driving & Fuel (Scope 1)

Direct fleet mileage and fuel combustion. Gasoline ~0.192 kg/km, Diesel ~0.171 kg/km, EV ~0.053 kg/km grid average.

Home & Office Power (Scope 2)

Electricity grid draw adjusted by regional factors: US (0.385 kg/kWh), EU (0.255 kg/kWh), India (0.710 kg/kWh), Global (0.475 kg/kWh).

Travel, Water & Digital (Scope 3)

Flight travel (with 1.9x radiative forcing multiplier), municipal water treatment lifecycle, and internet data transfer.

Section 3

Mathematical & Statistical Formulations

1. Holt-Winters Time-Series Forecasting

y_hat(t+h) = level(t) + h * trend(t) + seasonal(t+h-m)

Projects 12-month carbon trajectories with 95% confidence error bounds around the trend line.

2. Constrained Linear Programming Optimization

min sum(c_i * x_i) subject to sum(e_i * x_i) >= E_target

Calculates Pareto-optimal slider positions to maximize carbon reduction for a user's budget.

3. Z-Score Anomaly & Shapley Attribution

Z = (Current - Mean) / StdDev; Contribution_k = Delta_k / TotalDelta

Triggers alerts for consumption spikes (Z > 2.0) and decomposes variance drivers.
