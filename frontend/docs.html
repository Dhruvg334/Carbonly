Standardized GHG Protocol equations, statistical anomaly algorithms, Groq LLM integration, and REST API reference.

Section 1

Deterministic GHG Accounting Engine

Carbonly calculates emissions deterministically following global standards (GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, DEFRA, and EPA guidelines):

Emissions (kg CO2e) = Activity Data × Verified Emission Factor

Scope 1: Direct Emissions

Fleet transport mileage and direct fuel combustion. Gasoline ~0.192 kg/km, Diesel ~0.171 kg/km, EV ~0.053 kg/km grid average.

Scope 2: Purchased Electricity

Adjusted by regional power grid carbon intensity: US (0.385 kg/kWh), EU (0.255 kg/kWh), India (0.710 kg/kWh), Global (0.475 kg/kWh).

Scope 3: Value Chain & Travel

Commercial flights (with 1.9x radiative forcing multiplier), water supply/treatment lifecycle, and digital data transfer.

Section 2

Statistical Anomaly Detection (Z-Score & IQR)

To identify unexpected energy or transport spikes, the engine calculates the time-series Z-score over user historical entries:

Z = (Current Emissions - Mean Baseline) / Standard Deviation

Entries exceeding Z > 2.0 trigger an automated anomaly alert and invoke Groq AI for root-cause diagnosis.
